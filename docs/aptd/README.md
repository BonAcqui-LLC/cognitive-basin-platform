The canonical expansion from paper v1.0 forward is **APTD = Autonomic Persistence and Transition Device**.

Earlier dated materials used **APTD = ATAL Portable Training Devices**. That historical name and its training-device context remain part of the lineage. They are not rewritten or made to appear to have used the later expansion. The v1.0 name records a versioned widening from ATAL exposure and training toward an autonomic runtime, persistence substrate, physical interface, recovery boundary, and governed transition layer.

## System boundary

APTD is a proposed **runtime and physical boundary**, not the intelligence.

- A cognition layer may interpret evidence and propose an action.
- APTD is responsible for typed physical observation, provenance, qualification boundaries, durable continuity, recovery, governance, receipts, and bounded transition toward consequence.
- Observation does not grant persistent influence, and persistent influence does not grant action authority.
- The model is not root execution authority.

The `PERCEPT`, `ATAL`, `RIGOR`, `CIRCUIT`, `GUARD`, and `SERA` names describe historical or functional responsibilities. Fractalish v4 module prototypes do not constitute the FormationCore authority/history implementation, and these functions need not be separately deployed services. FormationCore remains the relevant Baby AI evidence line for historical retention, current-authority change, and continuity behavior.
